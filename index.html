Atomic Reps

Web, coding agents, Slack, short courses

One short question a day about the tools you use as a software engineer, asked again later so you keep the answer. It also works inside coding agents: after you change code with an agent, it asks you about what you just did.

How it works
You answer from memory, then see why the answer is right. Questions you get wrong come back sooner.
Where it runs
The web, a command-line tool and MCP server inside your coding agent, and a daily question in Slack.

Croonia

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android. Free.

A singing coach. You sing, and the app draws your pitch over the notes you are aiming for. After each run it tells you which phrases were in tune and what to practise next.

One session
A voice check-in, a straw warm-up, a range check, an exercise, and one song moved into your key.
Results
In cents and milliseconds: how far off a note was, and how late.

croonia.com

Croonia

Each visit is one guided session built from your own measurements:

  1. a voice check-in
  2. a short warm-up with a straw
  3. a range check
  4. an exercise
  5. work on one song, moved into your key

Results are given in cents and milliseconds, so you can see how far off a note was and how late.

Built with
SwiftUI for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android.

RockTune

Windows 10 and 11. Free and open source.

Windows tuning for games. It reads your CPU, graphics card and network adapter, then builds a PowerShell script with the settings that change frame times, input delay and network delay on that hardware.

Safe to try
Every run makes a restore point and records each value it changes. -Undo puts everything back; -DryRun only prints.
Also included
Rig Check for setup mistakes, Play Snapshot for background load, and a repeatable benchmark.

rocktune.pedroferrari.com

RockTune

You choose your hardware and games on the website, and it builds a script for that machine. The script changes power plans, timer resolution, MSI mode, MMCSS, GPU scheduling, network adapter settings and AMD X3D core parking. It also turns off overlays and background apps that run during a game, and installs your gaming software and peripheral apps.

Every run makes a System Restore point first and records each value it changes, so -Undo can put everything back. -DryRun prints every change without applying it.

Also included
Rig Check: finds setup mistakes that cost frames, such as RAM below its rated speed, a monitor below its highest refresh rate, or the display cable in the motherboard port.
Play Snapshot: lists what uses CPU and disk in the background while you play.
Benchmark: installs a repeatable test set, so you can compare frame times before and after.
Built with
PowerShell, Svelte, TypeScript, Vite
History
It grew out of RockInstaller from 2018.

Master Avatar

Web, in Portuguese and English

The website of Mestre Avatar Aang Willians, a 5th-degree kung fu master in São Paulo. It includes The Scroll, a 36-minute film about the history of Chinese martial arts, drawn live in the browser.

The Scroll
A prologue and 25 chapters, drawn on a canvas in the style of shadow puppets, cut paper and ink wash.

mestreavatar.com

Mestre Avatar Aang Willians

The site covers the master, his lineage (Grandmaster Chan Kowk Wai), Chen Village, an atlas of Chinese martial styles, weapons, a gallery and online class sign-up.

O Pergaminho (The Scroll) has a prologue and 25 chapters: the history of Chinese martial arts, Shaolin, Chinese medicine, Chen taiji, and the line that brought kung fu to Brazil. It is drawn on a canvas in the style of shadow puppets, cut paper and ink wash. Chapter one plays on the home page the first time you visit.
